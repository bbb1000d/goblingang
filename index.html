Goblin Gang

Mini side-scrolling action platformer inspired by Goblin Sword.

Controls

  • A / D or ← / → — move
  • W or Space — jump (double jump enabled)
  • J or K — sword attack
  • R — restart current level

Campfire Upgrades

Spend coins collected in the previous level.
